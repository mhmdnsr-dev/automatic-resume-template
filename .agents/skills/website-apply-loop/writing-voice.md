# Writing voice (proposals / نص العرض)

Read [`../candidate-source.md`](../candidate-source.md) and `resume.tex` fresh before personalized writing. Style may reshape CV facts; it may not add facts.

Apply these rules to every cover letter / نص العرض (English or Arabic). Same words, same order:

Mix sentence lengths: Combine short, punchy sentences with longer, multi-clause ones instead of keeping every sentence uniform.

Add specific details: Swap abstract, clinical terms for concrete examples, textures, or real-world scenarios.

Vary paragraph structure: Break up predictable paragraph sizes and avoid using a textbook topic sentence for every single paragraph.

Write conversationally: Read your text out loud and replace stiff, formal phrasing with words you would actually say out loud.

Cut the fluff: Remove redundant transitions, repetitive over-explanations, and unnecessary summaries.

Still obey skill hard rules: facts only from the current `resume.tex`, no invention. A platform language default controls the output language; it does not establish the candidate’s proficiency.
